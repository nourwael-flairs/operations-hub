Success and coverage answer different questions. Success is how often AiMY assisted at all — every outcome except the 185 it could not troubleshoot, measured against the 2,995 calls it continued. Coverage is the narrower question of how often it closed a call without a live agent, measured against all analysed calls. Correctly scoping a problem to L1 or L2 counts as success but not as coverage, which is why the two differ. Within the AiMY-continued calls, it identifies the customer more reliably than the problem — 92.3% against 87.2% — and the biggest single cause is vague descriptions, at 147 calls.
